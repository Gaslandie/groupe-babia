Detailed sectors

A diversified group for national development

Groupe Babia Guinea SARLU operates in seven complementary sectors designed to support food security, industrialization, infrastructure and trade.

01

Agriculture

Modern agriculture for food security. We invest in farming techniques, mechanization and irrigation to increase productivity.

  • Crop production: rice, maize, vegetables and cash crops.
  • Supply of agricultural inputs: seeds, fertilizers and equipment.
  • Support and training for farmers and cooperatives.
  • Land development and soil management.

Goal: reduce food imports and increase income for Guinean farmers.

View agri-food page
02

Agribusiness

Structuring agricultural value chains. We connect farmers to markets, financing and technology.

  • Project structuring and financing for agricultural enterprises.
  • Collection, storage and distribution of agricultural products.
  • Partnerships with cooperatives and agri-SMEs.
  • Market access and contract farming programs.

Goal: transform agriculture from subsistence to profitable business.

Explore value chains
03

Agro-industries

Adding value to Guinean products. We process local raw materials into finished goods for domestic and export markets.

  • Processing of cereals, fruits and vegetables.
  • Packaging and branding of "Made in Guinea" products.
  • Development of food processing units.
  • Quality control and compliance with international standards.

Goal: reduce post-harvest losses and create industrial jobs.

View agro-industry
04

Fishing

Sustainable fisheries & export. We promote responsible fishing and develop the cold chain to supply quality fish products.

  • Artisanal and industrial fishing operations.
  • Fish processing, freezing and packaging.
  • Cold storage and logistics.
  • Export of fishery products to regional and international markets.

Goal: ensure food security and earn foreign exchange through exports.

View fishing activity
05

Construction & BTP

Building infrastructure for Guinea. We design and execute public and private infrastructure projects to modern standards.

  • Modern markets, schools and administrative buildings.
  • Roads, drainage and urban development.
  • Residential and commercial buildings.
  • Renovation and maintenance services.

Flagship focus: Modern Market for Women, Gbessia, Conakry.

Goal: deliver quality infrastructure that improves daily life.

View construction page
06

Mining

Support services for the mining sector. We provide essential services to Guinea's mining industry.

  • Logistics and transport services.
  • Supply of equipment, materials and consumables.
  • Site preparation and civil works.
  • Subcontracting and facility management for mining companies.

Goal: be a reliable local partner for major mining operators in Guinea.

View mining page
07

Import & export

Connecting Guinea to the world. We ensure the availability of quality products in Guinea and promote "Made in Guinea" products internationally.

  • Import essential food products and construction materials.
  • Export Guinean agricultural products to generate value for farmers.
  • Handle sourcing, customs, logistics and distribution.
  • Operate with compliance to international standards.

Goal: facilitate trade for growth.
