Our vision & values

Driving industrialization, food security and sustainable prosperity

Groupe Babia Guinée SARLU aims to contribute to a Guinea that transforms its resources, creates jobs locally and builds growth that benefits communities.

Vision

To be West Africa's leading diversified group driving industrialization, food security and sustainable prosperity.

Vision

A national ambition with a West African outlook

We envision a Guinea where no one goes hungry, young people build careers at home, resources create local wealth, and growth remains sustainable for communities and the environment.

"We are not just building a company. We are building a nation."
Alhassane Doukoure, Founder & CEO
Mission

Transform natural resources into products, infrastructure and opportunities

Groupe Babia acts through integrated agriculture, industry and trade: from seed to shelf, from concept to construction and from resource to market.

Method

Integrate, industrialize, connect and impact

The group structures value chains, develops "Made in Guinea", builds international partnerships and creates local impact.

The Babia Way

The values that guide the group

Integrity

Transparent, ethical and compliant business. No shortcuts: trust is our currency.

Excellence

Global standards, from our 200 TPD rice mill to our solar systems: quality products, on-time delivery and continuous improvement.

Industrialization

Processing, packaging and adding value in Guinea instead of only trading raw materials.

Sustainability

70% renewable energy, zero-waste thinking, farmer support and profit with purpose.

People first

Our 350+ employees and 2000+ farmers are our greatest asset: training, safety and fair wages. "Guinean Talent, Global Impact".

Patriotism

Being 100% Guinean, reducing imports and making "Produit de Guinée" a source of pride.

Commitments

What the group promises its stakeholders

To Guinea

Reduce rice imports by millions of USD, create 350+ direct jobs and build infrastructure in Guinea and beyond.

To customers

Premium quality BABIA RICE and services: traceable, safe and affordable food for every Guinean.

To partners

A reliable partner for investors, banks and government, delivering what we promise.

To the environment

Integrate biomass, solar power, water recycling and progressive carbon footprint reduction.
