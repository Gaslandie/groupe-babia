Our identity

Groupe Babia Guinea

Groupe Babia Guinea brings together five complementary business areas. Agriculture connects supply chains and markets; agro-industry adds value to raw materials; construction supports infrastructure; the mining and fishing areas develop services and partnerships adapted to field realities.

Shared governance supports consistent commitments, the circulation of expertise and the building of durable relationships with our partners.
