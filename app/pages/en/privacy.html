Data protection

Privacy policy

This policy explains how the information sent to Groupe Babia Guinea is used in the context of contact and quotation requests.

Data concerned

When you prepare a request, you may provide your name, your company, your email address, your phone number, the nature of your need and your message.

Use of the information

This information is used only to understand your request, reply to you, prepare a commercial offer and follow up on the professional relationship.

Transmission

The form prepares a message in your own messaging application. If you choose WhatsApp, your message is processed according to the terms of that service. The website does not directly store the information you enter in a database.

Cookies and analytics

The website currently uses no advertising cookies and no analytics tool. External services opened from the website may apply their own policies.

Your requests

For any question about your information, or to ask for it to be corrected or deleted, write to infobabiaguinee@gmail.com. The French privacy policy remains the reference version.
