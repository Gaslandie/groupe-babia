## About us

GROUPE BABIA GUINÉE SARLU is a leading diversified Guinean company headquartered in
Kalou Conakry. we are committed to accelerating Guinea's economic transformation
through strategic investments in key growth sectors.

With a strong local presence and an international outlook, we combine Guinean expertise
with global standards of quality, governance, and execution. Our integrated business
model allows us to create value across the entire supply chain — from farm to market,
from concept to construction, from resource to export.

At Groupe Babia, we believe that sustainable development starts with local impact.
That is why every project we undertake is designed to create jobs, transfer skills,
and improve the livelihoods of the communities we serve.

## Our vision & values

### OUR VISION

To be West Africa's leading diversified group driving industrialization, food security,
and sustainable prosperity.

We envision a Guinea where:
- No one goes hungry - Guinea feeds itself and exports to the region
- Jobs are created at home - Our youth build careers in industry, not abroad
- Resources create wealth - From farm to factory, value is added in Guinea
- Growth is sustainable - Business thrives alongside communities and environment

« We are not just building a company. We are building a nation. »
— Alhassane Doukoure, Founder & CEO

### OUR MISSION

To transform Guinea's natural resources into high-quality products, infrastructure,
and opportunities through integrated agriculture, industry, and trade.

We do this by:
1. Integrating Value Chains: From seed to shelf, from mine to market
2. Industrializing Locally: Using technology and renewable energy to deliver "Made in Guinea"
3. Partnering Globally: Bringing investment and expertise while keeping control Guinean
4. Impacting Communities: Creating 1000s of jobs and supporting 2000+ farmers

### OUR CORE VALUES - THE BABIA WAY

1. INTEGRITY — We do business the right way. Transparent, ethical, and compliant.
   No shortcuts. Trust is our currency with partners, government, and communities.
2. EXCELLENCE — We build to global standards. From our 200TPD rice mill to our solar
   systems. Quality products, on-time delivery, continuous improvement.
3. INDUSTRIALIZATION — We don't just trade raw materials. We process, package, and add
   value in Guinea. That is how we create real wealth and jobs.
4. SUSTAINABILITY — 70% renewable energy. Zero waste. Supporting farmers. We grow today
   without compromising tomorrow. Profit with purpose.
5. PEOPLE FIRST — Our 350+ employees and 2000+ farmers are our greatest asset. We invest
   in training, safety, and fair wages. "Guinean Talent, Global Impact".
6. PATRIOTISM — We are 100% Guinean. "Produit de Guinée" is our pride. We are committed
   to reducing imports and putting Guinea on the map.

### OUR COMMITMENTS

- TO GUINEA — Reduce rice imports by millions USD. Create 350+ direct jobs.
  Build infrastructure in Guinea and beyond.
- TO OUR CUSTOMERS — Premium quality "BABIA RICE" and services. Traceable, safe,
  and affordable food for every Guinean.
- TO OUR PARTNERS — Be a reliable partner for investors, banks and government.
  Deliver what we promise.
- TO THE ENVIRONMENT — Biomass power, Solar, Water recycling. We measure our carbon
  footprint and reduce it every year.

## Our sectors

OUR 7 STRATEGIC SECTORS — Diversified to build a stronger Guinea

From the farm to the factory, from the port to the construction site — we operate across
the key industries driving Guinea's economic growth.

A DIVERSIFIED GROUP FOR NATIONAL DEVELOPMENT

GROUPE BABIA GUINÉE SARLU operates in 7 complementary sectors. This diversification
allows us to create synergies, manage risk, and deliver integrated solutions that have
maximum impact on Guinea's economy.

### 1. Agriculture — MODERN AGRICULTURE FOR FOOD SECURITY

We invest in modern farming techniques, mechanization, and irrigation to increase productivity.

1. Crop production: rice, maize, vegetables, and cash crops
2. Supply of agricultural inputs: seeds, fertilizers, equipment
3. Support and training for farmers and agricultural cooperatives
4. Land development and soil management

Goal: Reduce food imports and increase income for Guinean farmers.

### 2. Agribusiness — STRUCTURING AGRICULTURAL VALUE CHAINS

We connect farmers to markets, financing, and technology.

1. Project structuring and financing for agricultural enterprises
2. Collection, storage, and distribution of agricultural products
3. Partnerships with cooperatives and agri-SMEs
4. Market access and contract farming programs

Goal: Transform agriculture from subsistence to a profitable business.

### 3. Agro-Industries — ADDING VALUE TO GUINEAN PRODUCTS

We process local raw materials into finished goods for domestic and export markets.

1. Processing of cereals, fruits, and vegetables
2. Packaging and branding of "Made in Guinea" products
3. Development of food processing units
4. Quality control and compliance with international standards

Goal: Reduce post-harvest losses and create industrial jobs.

### 4. Fishing — SUSTAINABLE FISHERIES & EXPORT

We promote responsible fishing and develop the cold chain to supply quality fish products.

1. Artisanal and industrial fishing operations
2. Fish processing, freezing, and packaging
3. Cold storage and logistics
4. Export of fishery products to regional and international markets

Goal: Ensure food security and earn foreign exchange through exports.

### 5. Construction & BTP — BUILDING INFRASTRUCTURE FOR GUINEA

We design and execute public and private infrastructure projects to modern standards.

1. Construction of modern markets, schools, and administrative buildings
2. Roads, drainage, and urban development
3. Residential and commercial buildings
4. Renovation and maintenance services

Flagship Focus: Modern Market for Women - Gbessia, Conakry

Goal: Deliver quality infrastructure that improves daily life.

### 6. Mining — SUPPORT SERVICES FOR THE MINING SECTOR

We provide essential services to Guinea's mining industry.

1. Logistics and transport services
2. Supply of equipment, materials, and consumables
3. Site preparation and civil works
4. Subcontracting and facility management for mining companies

Goal: Be a reliable local partner for major mining operators in Guinea.

### 7. Import & Export — CONNECTING GUINEA TO THE WORLD

We ensure the availability of quality products in Guinea and promote "Made in Guinea"
products to regional and international markets.

FACILITATING TRADE FOR GROWTH

As part of our diversified activities, GROUPE BABIA GUINÉE SARLU operates a dedicated
Import & Export division.

Our mission:
1. Import essential food products and construction materials to support Guinea's
   population and industries
2. Export Guinean agricultural products to create foreign exchange and value for
   local farmers

We handle sourcing, customs, logistics, and distribution with full compliance to
international standards.
